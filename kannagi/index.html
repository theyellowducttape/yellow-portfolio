Story & Inspiration

A grieving wife walks into a king's court to avenge her husband's death and burns a kingdom to ashes

KANNAGI is a retelling of the final moments of The Silappathikaram, the Tamil epic of chastity, injustice and divine wrath written by Ilango Adigal. The retelling was imagined to make the viewers feel the weight of gold, the heat of the pyre, the silence before the anklet breaks.

When her husband, Kovalan, is wrongly executed for a theft he did not commit, Kannagi storms the court of the Pandya king Nedunchezhiyan to prove his innocence before the throne, then, in her grief and fury, casts down Madurai in flames.

Behind the Frame

The Pipeline

Pre-production

Script · Concept Art · Storyboard

The screenplay was written referencing different interpretations of the existing literature, the concept art and storyboard were drawn up to lock in the visual style and narrative of the final film.

Environment Design

Blender

The palace of Nedunchezhiyan, its pillared halls and high arches were modelled in Blender and textured in Unreal Engine. The grandeur of the palace was captured using references from ancient architectural structures still standing in present day Madurai.

Character Models

3D ScanMetaHuman

Live actors were 3D-scanned, then rebuilt and rigged as MetaHumans,giving the characters photoreal skin, believable eyes and a facial rig ready for performance capture.

Costume Design & Cloth Simulation

Marvelous Designer

Sarees, veils and court garments were patterned and simulated in Marvelous Designer so every fold moved with real weight, the draped silk carrying as much of the performance as the face.

Motion Capture

Xsens

Full-body performance was recorded with an Xsens inertial mocap suit, letting the actors play the courtroom plea and the final scenes in real time.

Voiceover

Studio Recording

All of the dialogues were recorded separately from the mocap performance, giving each voice actor room to find the courtroom plea and the final fury purely through the moment.

Post-production

After Effects

Render passes were composited in After Effects, fire, smoke, embers and atmosphere layered together, cleanup paint and finishing touches bringing every frame to its final read.
